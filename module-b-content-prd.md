## 1. Content Strategy

### 1.1 Voice and Tone

**Governing principle:** Write as a real person, not a studio.

The copy should feel like it was written by someone who has thought carefully about what they are doing and why — and is not trying to impress you.

**What this means in practice:**

- **Warm, but not casual.** There is care behind every sentence. Not friendliness for its own sake.
- **Precise, but not cold.** Specificity earns trust. Jargon does the opposite.
- **No buzzwords. No mission statement language. No AI hype vocabulary.** If a sentence would fit on a startup homepage from 2023, rewrite it.
- **The test:** Could you say this sentence out loud to someone you respect? If yes, keep it. If it sounds like a deck slide, cut it.

**What to avoid specifically:**
- "transformative," "cutting-edge," "leverage," "synergy," "unlock," "empower"
- AI hype language: "the future of," "AI-powered everything," "intelligent automation"
- Studio voice: "we believe," "our mission is," "we are passionate about"

**Bilingual approach:** The site is EN/JP. Japanese copy is not a translation of English — each version is written to read naturally in that language. Japanese uses a formal-but-accessible register: です/ます form in body copy; plain form in headers and labels.
